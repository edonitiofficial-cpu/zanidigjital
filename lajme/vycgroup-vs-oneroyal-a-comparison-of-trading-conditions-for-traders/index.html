VYCgroup vs. OneRoyal - A Comparison of Trading Conditions for Traders

ShpërndajeFacebookWhatsApp

Comparing VYCgroup and OneRoyal is interesting because these brokers are quite different from each other. One was established in 2022, uses proprietary trading software, and requires a minimum deposit of $5,000. The other has been operating since 2006, offers MetaTrader 4, and accepts clients with deposits starting from $50.

The first broker does not currently hold a license (according to publicly available information, its application is being processed), while the European division of the second broker is regulated by CySEC.

In this review, we will compare the two companies in terms of regulation, available instruments, order execution models, trading platforms, and trading costs.

Two Brokers: A Brief Overview

VYCgroup is a brokerage company that provides clients with access to various financial markets. The brand is owned by VY CAPITAL UK LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom in 2022 at 202 Ladbroke Grove, London, W10 5LZ (company registration number 14214424).

Through its proprietary trading software, VYCgroup offers access to highly liquid assets across different classes in the form of CFDs.

OneRoyal is a trading platform established in 2006 that allows traders to access virtually all major financial markets. Trading is also conducted through CFDs.

The brand's legal entity is Royal Financial Trading Ltd, while its European division, Royal Financial Trading (Cy) Ltd, is registered in Cyprus under registration number HE349061. Its primary trading platform is MetaTrader 4.

There is a 16-year difference between the two brokers in terms of their market history. While the age of a company does not necessarily determine the quality of its services, it reflects their different development paths: OneRoyal has a long-established presence in the market, while VYCgroup is still building its track record.

Regulation: The Key Difference

The most significant difference between the two brokers concerns their regulatory status.

VYCgroup currently does not hold a regulatory license, as its application is still being processed. According to available information, the company submitted its documents to the UK's Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) in 2026, and the application is under review .

The licensing process involves a thorough assessment of the company. Until it is completed, VYCgroup cannot be considered a regulated broker. Its regulatory status is currently listed as "None."

OneRoyal's European division, on the other hand, holds CySEC license No. 312/16, issued on August 10, 2016. The broker is also a member of the Investor Compensation Fund (ICF).

What does this mean for traders?

Holding a regulatory license means that a company is supervised by a financial authority and must comply with its requirements.

Submitting a license application does not provide the same status. Until the regulator makes a decision, it remains only an application.

This distinction does not automatically determine the quality of either broker's services, but regulatory status is often one of the first factors traders consider when choosing a financial intermediary.

It is also important to remember that a license belongs to a specific legal entity. In OneRoyal's case, the license applies to its European division, and the level of investor protection depends on the jurisdiction under which the client is registered.

Minimum Deposit and Range of Trading Instruments

The minimum deposit at VYCgroup is $5,000, compared to $50 at OneRoyal — a difference of 100 times.

OneRoyal therefore offers a more accessible entry point for traders. However, generating meaningful returns from a very small initial investment may be challenging.

VYCgroup offers leverage ranging from 1:10 to 1:100, depending on the account type.

OneRoyal provides leverage of up to 1:30 for traders from Europe and Australia, while clients from other regions may have access to leverage of up to 1:1000.

Both brokers support the same account currencies: USD and EUR.

In terms of the number of available trading instruments, VYCgroup is ahead of its competitors.

Its platform includes more than 3,000 assets, including:

Over 60 currency pairs

More than 1,500 stocks

22 stock indices

17 commodity instruments

More than 1,000 cryptocurrencies

Stocks and cryptocurrencies make up the largest part of its offering.

OneRoyal provides access to approximately 2,000 trading instruments.

These include Forex pairs, digital coins and tokens, stocks, stock indices, ETFs, oil, natural gas, gold, other metals, and agricultural commodities.

Both brokers cover the main asset classes: Forex, stocks, indices, commodities, and cryptocurrencies, all traded through CFDs.

OneRoyal places greater emphasis on securities.

Therefore, when choosing between the two companies, traders should consider not only the total number of available instruments but also whether the specific markets they intend to trade are supported.

Order Execution

The order execution model is another important difference between the brokers.

VYCgroup executes client orders using NDD (Non-Dealing Desk) technology.

Under this model, traders' orders are routed directly to the interbank market or external liquidity providers without the involvement of an internal dealing desk.

This approach removes the conflict of interest associated with internal dealing-desk execution.

Certain account types also offer ECN execution, providing access to major global liquidity providers.

OneRoyal also uses ECN execution for selected account types, while others operate under an STP model.

STP (Straight Through Processing) involves the automated processing and routing of orders through the execution infrastructure, reducing the need for certain manual intermediary operations.

According to the stated figures, the average order execution speed is:

VYCgroup: 132 ms

OneRoyal: 192 ms

The difference is 60 milliseconds in favor of VYCgroup.

However, these are average figures and do not necessarily reflect the execution speed of each individual trade. Actual performance depends on market conditions and other factors.

Trading Software

The brokers take fundamentally different approaches to trading technology.

VYCgroup clients trade through a proprietary web-based trading platform and a mobile application developed by the company.

Both versions offer essentially the same functionality.

The platform includes:

Interactive charts in several formats (line, bar, and candlestick)

Nine standard timeframes

Market, limit, and stop orders

More than 30 technical indicators

An economic calendar

Price alerts for selected assets

OneRoyal relies primarily on MetaTrader 4 (MT4) and its mobile version, including the MT4 Accelerator option.

The choice largely depends on individual trading preferences.

More traditional traders often prefer desktop versions of MetaTrader, while younger and more active users may appreciate the simplicity and accessibility of web-based trading solutions.

Spreads, Commissions, and Other Trading Costs

To compare trading expenses, we will examine three key parameters.

The minimum spread on EUR/USD starts from 0.6 pips at VYCgroup and 0.8 pips at OneRoyal.

Trading commissions are:

VYCgroup: $5 per lot

OneRoyal: €4 per lot

Both brokers apply standard swap charges.

It is important to remember that the advertised minimum spread does not mean every trade will be executed at that level.

Spreads depend on market conditions, liquidity, and account type. Higher-tier accounts typically offer tighter spreads.

Additionally, because commissions are denominated in different currencies, a precise cost comparison requires calculations based on a specific trade.

Customer Support, Deposits, and Withdrawals

VYCgroup provides customer support 24 hours a day, seven days a week (24/7).

OneRoyal offers support around the clock on business days (24/5).

This gives VYCgroup an additional advantage, particularly for cryptocurrency traders who may need assistance during weekends.

VYCgroup supports the following deposit methods:

Visa and Mastercard

Skrill

Neteller

Equals Money

Bank transfers

Cryptocurrencies, including BTC and stablecoins

Withdrawal requests are processed within 24 hours, although the broker charges a withdrawal fee of 10% of the amount.

This is a significant expense that traders should consider when comparing brokerage services.

OneRoyal supports approximately the same deposit and withdrawal methods.

However, withdrawal requests may take up to three days to process.

Information about OneRoyal's withdrawal fees is not available.

Among its additional services, VYCgroup offers educational materials, daily financial market analysis, and trading signals provided by the company's experts.

OneRoyal's additional offerings are primarily limited to market analysis published on weekdays.

Which Broker Is Better Suited to Different Traders?

VYCgroup may appeal to active traders with substantial trading capital.

Its high minimum deposit and leverage restrictions make it less suitable for beginners.

Although the broker provides educational resources and trading signals, these features do not eliminate the risks associated with financial trading.

OneRoyal is primarily aimed at traders who prefer investing in securities.

Its minimum deposit of $50 makes it more accessible to beginners, while MetaTrader 4 remains a familiar and widely used trading platform.

Final Comparison

VYCgroup's main advantages include:

Faster average order execution (132 ms versus 192 ms)

Lower minimum EUR/USD spreads (0.6 versus 0.8 pips)

A wider selection of trading instruments (3,000+ versus 2,000+)

Proprietary trading software with integrated services

However, the broker currently lacks a regulatory license, requires a relatively high minimum deposit of $5,000, and charges a substantial 10% withdrawal fee.

OneRoyal's advantages include its market presence since 2006, a European division regulated by CySEC under license No. 312/16, a low minimum deposit of $50, and the familiar MetaTrader 4 platform.

Its customer support schedule of 24/5 is somewhat less convenient.

There is no single winner in this comparison.

The right choice depends on individual priorities, whether those are a low entry threshold, execution speed, available instruments, or withdrawal processing times.

Before opening an account with either broker, traders should verify the latest trading conditions, regulatory status, and availability of services in their jurisdiction.
